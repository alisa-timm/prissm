Common and Specific Features in the Mythological Systems of Gagauz and Turks

Author:

Chubote Kristina

Category:

Research

Published:

05.08.2026

Topic:

Art and Culture

Language:

English

read

Keywords:

Gagauz mythology, Turkish mythology, Oghuz Turkic substrate, comparative folklore, religious syncretism, wolf cult, epic heritage

Abstract:

This research paper presents my comparative analysis of the mythological systems of the Gagauz and Turks, in which I identify common features and specific characteristics of folk traditions. My work combines elements of folklore studies, Turkology, and cultural anthropology, based on the analysis of oral narratives and archival materials.
