Prissm Research Platform Rules

1. Who Can Use the Platform

Any person who accepts these Rules may register and use the platform.

Authors may publish materials that they have created independently, including research papers, articles, or projects, provided they possess the necessary rights to publish them.

2. What Materials May Be Published

The platform welcomes:

  1. Research papers.
  2. Popular science articles.
  3. Analytical materials.
  4. Project works.

We support original ideas, critical thinking, and a respectful approach to science.

3. What Is Prohibited

The following is prohibited on the platform:

  1. Publishing someone else's work under your own name.
  2. Violating copyright.
  3. Posting materials containing insults, threats, or discrimination.
  4. Deliberately publishing false information.
  5. Using materials created entirely by artificial intelligence (AI).
  6. Publishing materials that violate the law.

The platform administration reserves the right to reject any work that violates these Rules.

4. Copyright

The author retains copyright to their work after publication.

By submitting material to the platform, the author confirms that the work was created independently or that they possess all necessary rights to publish it.

The author grants the platform the right to publish the material on the website, use it for the design of platform pages, publication on Prissm social media, and other materials related to promoting the project, with mandatory attribution to the author.

5. Material Moderation

Every submission undergoes a preliminary review.

During moderation, the following are evaluated:

  1. Compliance with the platform's subject areas.
  2. Compliance with these Rules.
  3. Absence of plagiarism.
  4. Proper formatting.

If necessary, the author may receive recommendations for improving the material and submit it again.

Successful moderation does not constitute a scientific peer review of the work. The author bears full responsibility for the content of the submitted materials.

6. Violation of the Rules

If these Rules are violated, the administration may:

  1. Issue a warning.
  2. Reject the publication.
  3. Temporarily restrict the user's capabilities.
  4. Block the account in the event of repeated or serious violations.

Each case is considered individually.

7. Changes to the Rules

The platform is constantly evolving, therefore these Rules may be amended, supplemented, and updated.

In the event of significant changes, the new version of the Rules will be published on the official Prissm website.

By continuing to use the platform after the Rules have been updated, the user confirms their acceptance of the revised version.
